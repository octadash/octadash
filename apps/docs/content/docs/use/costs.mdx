---
title: Costs
description: What each product costs to run (AI providers, servers, fixed bills), its margin against Money, a monthly forecast and budgets.
---

Costs reads what providers charge and counts the fixed bills you type, splits
them between products, and puts them against [Money](/use/money) for each
product's margin. Octadash never pays anything.

## Connect a cost

In a product, open **Costs** > **Set up** (or **Product settings** > **Areas** >
Costs).

- **OpenAI or Anthropic**: an admin key, used only to read costs and the list of
  projects. Octadash reads the last 90 days in the background, then every hour.
  Providers publish costs with a delay (Anthropic up to about 4 hours), so
  today's numbers grow during the day.
- **Fixed bill**, for what has no API (a server, a domain, a tool paid yearly):
  name, amount, currency, since when, every month or year. Counted day by day,
  so each month adds up to the bill.

## Shared costs

One AI account or one server often serves several products. Each product takes
the provider's projects that are its own, and a part of the rest, split:

- **Equally** between the products sharing it;
- **By traffic**: each product's share of that day's visitors, from
  [Traffic](/send-data/traffic);
- **Fixed percent**, scaled to add up to 100.

A change holds from today on: past days keep the split they had, so past
margins do not move.

## What it shows

- **Spend** in the period (this month, 7 or 30 days) against the period before,
  and spend per day.
- **Forecast this month**: spent so far, plus the daily average of the last 7
  days for each day left.
- **Margin**: revenue from Money (paid minus refunded) less the costs, out of
  the revenue. Products without revenue show no margin.
- **AI today** against its daily average, and the product furthest above its
  own.
- **Budgets**: a monthly budget per product with a warning percent. The page
  warns when the forecast reaches it. Spend at 3× normal for an hour is the
  **Cost runaway** emergency, budget or not.

Amounts are in the organization's currency; other currencies are converted at
the day's rate.

## AI calls from your apps

Apps that report their model calls (OpenTelemetry `gen_ai` spans, PostHog AI
events) get one more table: calls, tokens and cost per product and model, at
list price. It is how a shared AI account shows which product and model spent
it. See [AI calls](/send-data/ai).

## Ask your AI

With a token that reads Costs, the [MCP server](/use/mcp) answers `costs`:
spend, revenue and margin for a period against the one before, the month's
forecast against budgets, AI today, per product and per provider.
