---
title: Money
description: Revenue, MRR, churn and failed payments of every product, read from Stripe, Paddle and Asaas with a read-only key.
---

Money reads payments and subscriptions from your payment gateways. It never
charges, refunds or changes anything.

## Connect a gateway

In a product, open **Money** > **Set up** (or **Product settings** > **Areas** >
Money), pick the gateway and follow the steps.

1. **Key.** Octadash checks it when you save and stores it encrypted.

   | Gateway | Key                                                                                                         |
   | ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
   | Stripe  | A restricted key (`rk_…`) with Read only. A secret key (`sk_…`) is refused, since it can charge and refund. |
   | Paddle  | A key with Read on transactions, subscriptions and products, and nothing else.                              |
   | Asaas   | Asaas has no read-only keys. Create one just for Octadash and delete it in Asaas to cut access at once.     |

2. **Which payments are this product's.** All of the account, only some of the
   gateway's products, or by metadata (`product` in Stripe and Paddle, the
   external reference in Asaas). One account can serve several products without
   counting anything twice.
3. **Live updates.** Add the webhook the page shows (Stripe: `charge.*` and
   `customer.subscription.*`; Paddle: `transaction.*` and `subscription.*`;
   Asaas: payment and subscription events, with the access token). Without it,
   Octadash reads every 5 minutes.
4. **History.** The last 12 months are imported in the background, so MRR and
   churn start with history.

## What it shows

- **Revenue** paid in the period, with the one-off part apart, and what was
  refunded.
- **MRR**: what every active subscription adds per month, at its price today.
  Yearly plans count a twelfth; trials only once they pay.
- **New subscriptions** and **churn** (subscriptions that ended, out of those
  active when the period started).
- **Failed payments**, and the emergency **Payments failing**: 20% of charges
  fail in 15 minutes, with at least 10 charges. Changeable in the product's
  alert rules.
- Customers who paid and who **bought again**, and the average sale.

Every amount is shown in the organization's currency (**Octadash settings** >
**General**); other currencies are converted at the day's rate. Margin, against
what the product costs, is in [Costs](/use/costs).

## Ask your AI

With a token that reads Money, the [MCP server](/use/mcp) answers `money`: the
same totals for a period against the one before, per product, with the latest
payments.
