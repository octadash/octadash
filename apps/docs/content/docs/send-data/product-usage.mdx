## Rules

- Event names in `snake_case`. Properties are optional.
- Never send names, e-mails or documents. They are dropped anyway.
- `signed_up` counts as a sign-up with no setting.
- `distinct_id` is the person. Octadash keeps only a hash of it.

## Sign-ups dropped

Once a product has 4 weeks of sign-ups, Octadash learns what is normal and
checks every hour whether the last 24 hours are well under it. That is a
signal, not an emergency: no message, a note on top of Product usage and a page
that says why.

Expected = shape × level. The level is the 7 days before; the shape is the part
of a week those 24 weekday hours held over the 4 weeks before. A quiet Sunday
expects few, and a campaign that lifted every day lifts what is expected too.
It is raised at 30% under the expected with at least 10 sign-ups expected, so a
small product going from 2 to 1 is not "−50%". Both numbers, and turning it
into an emergency that sends a message, are in **Alert rules**.

## How long events are kept

13 months by default; 3, 6 or 25 months in **Product settings** > **Data kept**
(one choice for Traffic and Product usage). A shorter choice deletes what is
older right away.

## PostHog

Your PostHog calls keep working: `capture` and `identify`. Autocapture,
recordings and surveys stay off. See [Coming from PostHog](/send-data/migrate#posthog).
