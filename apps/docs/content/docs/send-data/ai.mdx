---
title: AI calls
description: What each product spends on AI models, from the calls your app reports with OpenTelemetry.
---

Each call your app makes to a model (a chat, an embedding) can report its
model and tokens. Octadash reads them, prices them and shows them in
[Costs](/use/costs) per product and model. Nothing to wrap: the usual AI SDKs
already report them.

## Send the calls

Use any instrumentation that follows the OpenTelemetry `gen_ai` conventions
and export traces to Octadash with the product's source key:

```bash
OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT=https://ingest.example.com/otlp
OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_HEADERS=x-octadash-key=YOUR_SOURCE_KEY
OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_PROTOCOL=http/protobuf
```

- **Vercel AI SDK**: turn telemetry on in each call,
  `experimental_telemetry: { isEnabled: true }`, with an OpenTelemetry setup
  in the app (in Next.js, `registerOTel` from `@vercel/otel` in
  `instrumentation.ts`).
- **Python, Java, .NET and others**: the OpenTelemetry instrumentation for the
  OpenAI, Anthropic or Gemini client, or OpenLLMetry.
- **Sentry SDKs** with an AI integration send the same spans inside their
  transactions; they count too.

The spans themselves are kept with the other traces.

## PostHog AI events

`$ai_generation` and `$ai_embedding` events sent to the classic PostHog
endpoints count as calls too, with the cost PostHog's SDK sent when it sent
one. The newest PostHog server SDKs send them to a new endpoint Octadash does
not read yet, so for servers prefer OpenTelemetry.

## How it is priced

- The model and the tokens come from the call: input, output and cached input
  (read from or written to the provider's cache), each at its own price.
- The prices are the providers' list prices, from LiteLLM's price list,
  updated with Octadash. A model the list does not have keeps its tokens and
  shows "no price".
- A cost the app sent (PostHog's `$ai_total_cost_usd`) wins over the list.
- It is an estimate in US dollars. The bill of an AI account connected in
  Costs stays the total; the calls show which product and model spent it.

## Ask your AI

With a token that reads Costs, the [MCP server](/use/mcp) answers `ai_usage`:
calls, tokens and cost per product and model over the last days.
