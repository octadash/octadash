---
title: What Octadash is
description: One dashboard for all your products. Self-hosted and open source.
---

Octadash shows which of your products need you, why, since when, and what to do
next. Uptime, traffic, product usage, errors, money, costs and logs, for every
product you run, on one screen you host yourself.

<Callout type="warn">
  Octadash is in early development and not ready for production yet. Use the
  `edge` image (built from `main`) until the first release.
</Callout>

## What you get

- **All products at once.** Every area works for all products, for a group of
  products or for one product, on the same screen.
- **A product with only its name.** Its page shows every area with what it is
  for and how to set it up on your platform. Each card becomes that area's
  summary once data arrives.
- **Change one URL.** Point a Sentry SDK, a PostHog SDK or an OpenTelemetry
  exporter at Octadash and it works. See [Coming from Sentry, PostHog or OpenTelemetry](/send-data/migrate).
- **Uptime that never wakes your database.** Checks hit an endpoint that answers
  without a query, and alerts go out after 3 failures in a row.
- **Everything in the box.** One image plus the stores it installs and manages.
  You never open a database console.

## Areas

| Area          | Answers                                           | Needs                   |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Overview      | what needs my attention, and the day in numbers   | nothing: built from the others |
| Uptime        | is it up, is the certificate valid, did a job run | nothing to install      |
| Traffic       | who comes and from where                          | a script on the site    |
| Product usage | sign-ups, logins, key actions, funnel, retention  | browser or server code  |
| Errors        | what broke and for how many people                | any Sentry SDK          |
| Money         | revenue, MRR, churn, refunds                      | a Stripe, Asaas or Paddle key |
| Costs         | AI cost, infra cost, margin                       | provider keys or fixed bills |
| Logs          | what the server did at that moment                | any OpenTelemetry SDK   |
